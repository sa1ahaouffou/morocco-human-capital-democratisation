## Handling notes

- **Interpolation.** Barro-Lee is observed every five years. Annual values in between are linear interpolations and are flagged `bl_interpolated = TRUE`. Filter on it for observed-only work.
- **Spliced series.** `mys_best` joins Barro-Lee (ages 25–64) with UNDP (ages 25+). The two differ in definition; `mys_best_source` records which applies to each row.
- **Harmonised codes.** Barro-Lee's legacy codes ROM (Moldova) and SER (Serbia) are recoded to MDA and SRB; V-Dem's Kosovo (OWID_KOS) to the World Bank's XKX.
- **PISA** columns come from World Bank EdStats and stop in 2018; Morocco's PISA 2022 scores (365 / 339 / 365) are cited in the notes from the OECD, not stored here.
